# Best Fantasy Football Services for a CBS "Team QB" League (2026 Season)

## TL;DR

- **Primary pick: Draft Sharks (~$192/yr full-access annual) paired with your existing CBS Sports Fantasy league.** Draft Sharks is the only service that combines (a) independently verified top-tier accuracy — its analyst Kevin English won FantasyPros' 2024 Draft Accuracy title and it placed four analysts in the top 25 — (b) a genuine native iOS app with CBS live-draft sync, and (c) fully custom scoring/roster configuration. No third-party service natively supports a "Team QB" (TQB) roster slot, so you will build that yourself by summing each franchise's individual QB projections.
- **The Team QB slot is the decisive constraint: zero third-party tools model it.** CBS confirms TQB is "the cumulative totals of all quarterbacks on a given NFL team." You must draft QBs by NFL franchise (offense quality + starter mobility + backup competence), not by individual name. Use team-level QB point totals (FFToday, free) plus a subscription's individual QB projections summed by team.
- **Best combination: Draft Sharks (accuracy + app + CBS sync) + FantasyPros MVP ($71.88/yr) for consensus rankings and a second CBS-syncing draft assistant + free FFToday team-QB tables for the Team QB math.** Skip CBS's own premium tier — you already have the league and its value-add is thin.

## Key Findings

### 1. Accuracy / hit rate

- **FantasyPros runs the only long-standing, independent, third-party accuracy competition** (since 2010), scoring 200+ experts' final preseason rankings against actual end-of-season results, and separately tracking weekly in-season accuracy. It is the industry's de facto benchmark. Methodology: it sets a player-pool cutoff per position (25 for QB, 50 for RB, etc.), scores each expert's rank error, and combines QB+RB+WR+TE for the overall leaderboard (WR and RB dominate because their player pools are larger).
- **2024 draft accuracy: Kevin English of Draft Sharks finished #1 overall out of 225 experts** — 2nd at RB and 12th at WR, an "impressive resume that includes two other top-10 finishes, and ranking higher than the average expert in each of the past 10 years." Jody Smith (FullTime Fantasy) placed 2nd and Sean Koerner (The Action Network) 4th. Draft Sharks was the only site with four analysts in the top 25 and placed two (English, Jared Smola) in the multi-year top 10.
- **2023 draft accuracy: Christopher Dell of Betting Predators won overall**, besting a 236-expert field "in just his fourth year competing"; David Kluge of Footballguys placed 2nd. Jared Smola of Draft Sharks won FantasyPros' Multi-Year Draft Accuracy award that year.
- **2025 draft accuracy: Seth Miller of Crossroads Fantasy Football won overall** (out of 212 experts) by being the #1 WR ranker and #2 QB ranker; Guilherme Gianni (Fantasy Futebolista) was runner-up. Footballguys' Joey Wright finished 6th and Draft Sharks' Jody Smith finished 9th. No major-brand subscription site dominated — small independents took the top slots.
- **2025 in-season (weekly) accuracy: Justin Boone of Yahoo Fantasy won overall** — his second win (also 2019), a ninth top-10 finish, and the #1 WR ranker. Patrick Thorman of Establish The Run finished 2nd — "his fourth consecutive year finishing in the top 4, including being our #1 expert in 2022," with nine top-10 finishes and never placing lower than 24th.
- **Skeptical read on marketing claims:** Draft Sharks brands itself "verified most accurate," but that rests largely on English's single 2024 overall win plus multi-year top-10 placements — real and third-party, but one title, not perennial dominance. The overall winner changes every year (Dell 2023, English 2024, Miller 2025), so **no single provider is reliably "most accurate" every season.** The practical takeaway: lean on a consensus (FantasyPros ECR aggregates 100+ experts) rather than betting everything on one site's "most accurate" badge. PFF's "award-winning" and "data used by all 32 NFL teams" claims are about its grades/data being licensed to NFL teams (verifiable) — not about topping the accuracy competition. 4for4's "most accurate over the last 4 years" is a self-selected framing, not a FantasyPros overall title.

### 2. iOS / iPad apps

- **Draft Sharks:** Native iOS app; Draft War Room with live-draft sync to CBS (plus Yahoo, ESPN, Sleeper, NFL, MFL, RT Sports, Underdog, Fleaflicker, Fantrax); full projections, rankings, in-season tools; live sync and custom scoring available in-app.
- **FantasyPros ("Fantasy Advice" app):** Native, mature iOS app, updated for iOS 26. Its Draft Assistant with live sync supports Yahoo, **CBS**, NFL.com, MyFantasyLeague, RT Sports, Fantrax, and NFFC directly in-app (ESPN requires the desktop Chrome extension). This is the strongest pure draft-assistant app that syncs CBS.
- **Footballguys Draft Dominator:** Rebuilt for 2026; now free to download but requires a subscription to unlock; live draft sync now on every major host including CBS. It performs in-app VBD (value-based drafting) — the single most useful feature for a nonstandard scoring setup like Team QB.
- **PFF app:** Native iOS; Mock Draft Simulator, live draft assistant, start/sit optimizer, rankings; increasingly oriented toward betting props.
- **4for4 LeagueSync app:** Native iOS/Android; imports CBS rosters, optimal-lineup and waiver tools; more an in-season lineup app than a full draft assistant.
- **CBS Sports Fantasy app:** Native, 4.6-star average across 254,407 user reviews. This is your league-management app (draft, lineups, trades, live scoring) and it runs the Team QB slot itself, but it offers no advanced cross-site projection tooling.

### 3. Team QB / CBS support — the decisive criterion

- **CBS natively supports the Team QB (TQB) slot in its own Commissioner leagues.** CBS's glossary defines it: "TQB: Team Quarterback — The cumulative totals of all quarterbacks on a given NFL team. These are used to ensure that an owner has the starter and backup quarterbacks on the same squad." CBS also maintains a dedicated help article, "How does scoring for the Team Quarterback (TQB) position work?" The mechanic mirrors ESPN's TQB (documented verbatim by ESPN): you receive all points from whoever plays QB for your franchise, including the backup after an in-game injury.
- **No third-party ranking/projection service natively models Team QB.** None publish draftable "team-level QB rankings." Every service — Draft Sharks, FantasyPros, Footballguys, PFF, 4for4, ESPN, NFL.com, and CBS's own expert rankings — ranks individual quarterbacks only.
- **CBS league sync IS widely supported** — the good news. Draft Sharks, FantasyPros, Footballguys (2026), 4for4, and Fantasy Life all sync CBS leagues, so you can import your roster and settings; the tools simply won't understand the TQB slot and will treat your QBs as individuals.
- **The workaround (the real answer):** Build Team QB values by summing each franchise's individual QB projections from your subscription, then adjust for depth-chart stability and starter mobility. Cross-check against FFToday's free "Fantasy Points Scored: Quarterback" team tables, which already aggregate all of a franchise's QB production — exactly the TQB concept.
- **Which franchises produce the most/least Team QB value (historical, FFToday team-QB totals):**
  - 2024 top franchise QB units: **Ravens 447.2, Commanders 413.6, Bills 402.7, Bengals 400.6, Buccaneers 399.6.**
  - 2024 bottom: **Rams 256.4, Saints 257.4, Raiders 257.7, Texans 262.0, Falcons 262.4.**
  - 2025 top: **Bills 417.7, Rams 371.4, Patriots 370.9, Jaguars 366.1, Giants 348.4.**
  - 2025 bottom: **Titans 217.5, Browns 222.9, Raiders 226.4, Dolphins 230.9, Falcons 243.8.**
  - **Volatility lesson:** the Ravens' QB unit fell from #1 (447.2) in 2024 to #21 (284.7) in 2025 when Lamar Jackson missed time — proof that even an elite starter carries franchise-level injury risk. The TQB slot partially insures you (you keep the backup's points), but a weak backup on a one-man offense still craters your week.
- **Strategy implications of Team QB:** It eliminates in-week QB injury scrambling and makes QB handcuffing unnecessary (you already own the backup). It rewards franchises with (a) high-volume/mobile starters and (b) competent backups. It matters most in deep/Superflex/best-ball leagues; in a standard 10–12 team single-TQB league, QB depth still lets you wait on the position. Per the Footballguys forum consensus, TQB "protect[s] you more from injuries, since QBs typically score more than any other position."

## Details

### Draft Sharks — PRIMARY PICK

- **Why:** Best blend of verified accuracy (English #1 in 2024; multiple multi-year top-10 analysts), a real native iOS app, CBS live-draft sync, and full custom scoring/roster settings that let you replicate your exact CBS scoring (e.g., 4- vs 6-point passing TDs, 1 pt/25 pass yds). Its "3D Values" system recalculates player value live during the draft based on positional scarcity and your league rules.
- **For Team QB:** Provides 40+ individual QB rankings with floors/ceilings and a team filter, so you can sum QB projections by franchise quickly. It won't auto-create a TQB position, but custom scoring + team-filterable QB projections are the best raw material for building your own Team QB board.
- **Pricing (2026, draftsharks.com):** Tiered plans billed monthly/semiannual/annual. Full-access "Advice" tier is $44/mo (≈$192/yr equivalent); "Advanced" is $22/mo, "Redraft" is $16/mo; a top "Plus Personalized Advice" tier lists at $528. Money-back guarantee through Dec 31, 2026. Confirm exact tier names/prices at checkout — the pricing widget renders dynamically and could not be captured cleanly.

### FantasyPros MVP — STRONG ALTERNATE / COMPLEMENT

- **Why:** Runs the accuracy competition itself; its Expert Consensus Rankings aggregate 100+ experts (including Draft Sharks and PFF), the smart hedge against any one site's year-to-year variance. Its Draft Wizard app has the best CBS-syncing draft assistant among third parties.
- **For Team QB:** Custom scoring settings; build a QB cheat sheet and sum by team. Syncs CBS directly in the mobile app.
- **Pricing (2026, verified):** PRO $47.88/yr ($3.99/mo equiv), MVP $71.88/yr ($5.99/mo equiv), HOF $107.88/yr ($8.99/mo equiv). MVP is the sweet spot (adds Draft Assistant w/ Sync, keeper/IDP support, 10 live syncs).

### Footballguys — STRONG ALTERNATE

- **Why:** The rebuilt-for-2026 Draft Dominator does in-app VBD, which correctly values positions under nonstandard scoring — uniquely useful for pricing a Team QB slot. 2026 added live draft sync across every major host including CBS.
- **Pricing (2026, footballguys.com):** Monthly ~$9.99/mo (cancel anytime); Elite plan renews at $89.99/yr (formerly Hall of Fame $95.88). Live Draft Sync is now included on every plan for 2026.

### Others evaluated

- **PFF:** PFF+ $79.99 (early-bird through Aug 17; regularly $119.99). Strong projections and app; verify CBS sync before relying on it. Best for data depth, not Team QB.
- **4for4:** Classic $29/season, Pro ~$59/season (adds LeagueSync/Draft Hero). Syncs CBS. Good rankings, basic tools; custom scoring can approximate but not model TQB.
- **ESPN / ESPN+:** Free rankings (Mike Clay) are excellent, and ESPN publishes "team tiers/fantasy value by team" articles useful for Team QB context — but ESPN tools don't sync CBS.
- **Fantasy Life (Matthew Berry):** Generous free tier; syncs CBS. Content-strong, tooling lighter.
- **Establish The Run / Underdog:** Thorman is a perennial top-2 in-season accuracy performer, so ETR is worth it for weekly play, but it lacks CBS-specific and Team QB tooling.
- **RotoWire, RotoBaller, Sharp Football, FTN, Rotoviz, PlayerProfiler, Fantasy Points, Razzball, The Athletic, Sleeper tools, Yahoo Fantasy Plus, Dynasty Nerds:** All rank individual players only; none model Team QB. Yahoo Plus and ESPN+ are tied to their own platforms and don't help a CBS league.

### CBS's own premium offering

Not worth an extra purchase. You're already in a CBS league (free) that runs the TQB slot and gives you CBS's expert rankings and SportsLine-powered projections in-app. CBS's paid Commissioner upgrades are about league administration (custom scoring, keepers, salary cap), not superior projections.

## Recommendations

1. **Now (pre-draft): Subscribe to Draft Sharks annual (~$192).** Use its custom scoring to replicate your CBS scoring exactly, then build a Team QB board by summing each franchise's QB projections and ranking all 32 franchises.
1. **Add FantasyPros MVP ($71.88/yr)** for consensus rankings (a hedge against single-site variance) and as your in-app CBS-syncing draft assistant on draft day. Two independent CBS-syncing draft tools is cheap insurance if one fails to sync live.
1. **Use FFToday's free team-QB tables** to sanity-check your Team QB rankings against how franchises' QB units actually finished in 2024–2025, weighting toward teams with mobile starters and competent backups.
1. **Draft-strategy benchmarks for Team QB:** Target strong franchise QB units (Bills; rising units like the Patriots) but don't overpay — in a single-TQB 10–12 team league, positional depth means you can wait. Prioritize franchises whose backup wouldn't collapse the unit. Avoid perennial bottom-five franchises (Titans, Raiders, and Falcons were weak in both 2024 and 2025) unless the price is a steep discount.
1. **Thresholds that change the plan:** If your league is deep (14+ teams) or Superflex, escalate QB priority and add Footballguys for its VBD engine. If you play primarily weekly/in-season, add Establish The Run for Thorman's top-2 weekly accuracy. If Draft Sharks' checkout price exceeds ~$200 or its CBS sync fails in your pre-draft test, make FantasyPros MVP your primary and add Footballguys' VBD tool.

## Caveats

- **Team QB is niche and unmodeled by every third-party tool.** Any "Team QB rankings" you use will be self-built from individual QB projections — inference-heavy, layering backup-QB and depth-chart-volatility judgment on top of individual projections.
- **Draft Sharks pricing renders dynamically** and tier names/prices could not be captured cleanly; confirm at checkout. The ~$192 figure is the full-access annual equivalent inferred from listed monthly pricing ($44/mo "Advice" tier).
- **FFToday team-QB totals use FFToday's own QB scoring** (not CBS's exact settings) and cover the 17-game regular season only; rely on the rank order, not absolute totals. Under CBS scoring, totals will shift modestly.
- **Accuracy "winners" change yearly** (Dell 2023, English 2024, Miller 2025), so treat any "most accurate" marketing claim skeptically and lean on consensus.
- **PFF and 4for4 CBS-sync support should be verified** before you rely on them for a CBS league; both list CBS in some places but syncing behavior varies.
- **The CBS TQB help-article body could not be scraped** (JavaScript-rendered); the scoring mechanic is confirmed via CBS's own glossary definition and the identical, fully documented ESPN TQB slot.
- **Fact vs. inference:** Accuracy results, app details, pricing, CBS sync support, the CBS TQB definition, and the franchise QB totals are all verified from sources. The specific Team QB draft-round/ADP guidance and franchise-targeting advice are my inference built on those facts, because no service publishes Team QB ADP.

**Implications:**

1. Draft Sharks **natively emitted draftable team-level QB rankings** for this exact
   league. The "sum each franchise's QB projections by hand and layer on backup-QB
   judgment" workaround — the most laborious and error-prone part of the plan — is
   unnecessary if 2026 behaves like 2024.
2. This *strengthens* the Draft Sharks recommendation rather than weakening it, and
   removes the report's largest stated caveat.
3. **Verify before purchase:** confirm 2026 CBS sync still emits TQB. This is the single
   question that most affects the ~$192 decision.
4. **Counter-caveat:** the `Auction $` column in that same file is broken — every row,
   including all 32 TQB rows, reads `$-11`. The companion export has real values. So DS's
   TQB *projections* are trustworthy; its TQB *auction values* need independent
   verification.

The claim may still hold for FantasyPros and Footballguys — it was not tested against
their extracts.
